In Class Project

This semester in Technology A was my first experience learning to code, and it opened up an entirely new way of thinking about systems, storytelling, design, and data. At its core, this class became about exploring the connective tissue of the world around us: the intangible systems and rhythms that quietly shape daily life, made visible through data, interaction, and technology.

My first project transformed the manual for a tide clock I had repaired into a website, balancing the tiny mechanical details of coding and clock systems with the larger celestial rhythms that govern the device. From there, I created an interactive world map visualizing New York City's food imports. For my final project, Drawing Time, I built an interactive camera using physical computing to record and composite data over a set period, creating a portrait of a specific space and moment in time.
