The Looking Glass

An interactive fogged mirror for the browser

Format

Camera / Canvas / Gesture

Focus

Reflection / Obstruction / Reveal

Interactive Mirror

The Looking Glass is an interactive browser piece that behaves like a fogged mirror. The page requests access to the user-facing camera, mirrors and softens the video feed, then covers it with a layer of condensation.

Dragging across the surface wipes the fog away, creating temporary openings through the glass. The piece explores intimacy, reflection, obstruction, and reveal through a direct physical gesture.
