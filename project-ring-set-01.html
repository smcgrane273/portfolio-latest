Jewelry / Object Study

Ring Set 01

July 2026

Reconstituted sterling silver

Circle + rectangle

A pair of rings fabricated from reconstituted sterling silver, exploring material, geometry, and form. Silver is one of the few materials that can be endlessly recycled without losing its properties, moving through extraction, manufacture, use, recovery, melting, and reforming before becoming something new.

Beginning with standard industrial silver stock, the collection transforms two fundamental geometries, a circle and a rectangle, into wearable objects. Designed to be worn together, the rings create a quiet dialogue between symmetry and difference. The subtle shift from a circular to a square profile changes the way light, shadow, and weight are perceived.
