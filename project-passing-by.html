Passing By

A photo book built from images made in motion: road light, night landscapes, passing views, and the blur of looking from inside a moving frame.

Format

Photo Book

Focus

Movement / Image / Sequence

Context

Passing By gathers images that feel caught between arrival and disappearance: landscapes seen through a window, road scenes softened by speed, and night images where light becomes a trace instead of a fixed subject.

The book uses sequence to hold those fleeting views together. Rather than treating each photograph as a single destination, the project is about the feeling of moving through images before they fully settle.

Process

The contact sheet makes the project's source material visible: strips of road, horizon, shadow, and partial frames that were edited into a book sequence.
